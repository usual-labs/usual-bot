# Code of Conduct

Usual Bot is built by people with different backgrounds, experience and opinions. We want taking
part to be welcoming and free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality,
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This code applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when
you represent the project elsewhere.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person, and take feedback the same way.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to the people they affected, and learn from them.
- Put what's best for the people using Usual Bot ahead of winning an argument.

## What isn't acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Insults, slurs, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private, including continuing to contact someone who asked you to
  stop.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address, a token or a conversation,
  without their explicit permission.
- Anything else a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour breaks this code, or makes you uncomfortable, tell the maintainers
privately: use the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or GitHub's **Report
content** option on the comment or issue. Every report is read and handled confidentially, and we
won't tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

## What happens next

Maintainers decide how to respond, in proportion to what happened:

1. **A private note** explaining what was wrong, for a first or minor problem.
2. **A warning**, with a period in which to stay away from the people involved.
3. **A temporary ban** from the project's spaces, for serious or repeated problems.
4. **A permanent ban**, for a pattern of harm or for harassment.

Maintainers who don't follow this code in good faith may be removed from the project.
