# Usual Bot — 50 assistant characters

All 50 character concepts were written before image generation began. These are creative avatar personas, not implementations of new app capabilities.

## 01. Pip — A paperclip concierge

- **Assistant role:** Organization.
- **Personality:** Precise, shy, quietly dependable.
- **Signature trait:** Straightens a pile before saying hello.
- **Visual:** A plump upright white paperclip creature, one big rounded loop body, charcoal inner loop, two tiny eyes on a short white bridge, a small folded gray collar at its base, leaning forward politely.
- **Avatar:** [01-pip.png](png/01-pip.png)
