# Reference study

Both supplied sheets were inspected individually before character development.

## Sheet 1

Twenty motifs, read left to right, top to bottom: bending pencil; sparkling cloud; helmeted helper; sprouting laboratory tube; balance scales; key; light bulb; spanner; craft knife; bubbling flask; little notebook; coding laptop; satellite dish; folding laptop; waving flag; paintbrush; loose document; paper tray/printer; rocket; chef hat.

This sheet gives useful tools a quiet personality. The face is usually two tiny vertical dots. Tilt, curve, and silhouette do more work than facial expression. White main fills, gray secondary surfaces, and charcoal outlines remain distinct against yellow. Small black grounding shapes keep the figures from floating. The yellow is presentation background, not part of our PNGs.

## Sheet 2

Nineteen illustrated motifs, followed by the wordmark: button-pressing glove; game controller; cat emerging from a box; drooping ghostlike flower; fallen ice-cream cone; magnifying glass; server stack; flashlight; flaming wastebasket; peeled banana; wind-up duck; apple; flaming mug; cash register; avocado; circular nib emblem; articulated robot arm; pen nib with control handles; friendly robot.

This sheet expands into creature/object hybrids and small comic incidents. Eyes sometimes move into a dark face panel or an opening. Asymmetric poses, one accessory, and occasional motion marks convey mood. Gray remains a flat accent rather than shaded realism.

## Direction for the new family

Original object-creature hybrids; monochrome charcoal/white/gray; gently irregular rounded linework; minimal faces; one dominant silhouette and at most one defining accessory. Full characters centered in a square with safe transparent margins. No yellow background, text, labels, scenery, checkerboard, glossy rendering, or faux 3D. Every asset is generated separately, never extracted from a crowded sheet. Preserve opaque white bodies and genuine transparent surroundings.
